Cinnamon Roll Wreath with Apples

Recipe

Ingredients you'll need

  • Crescent roll dough
  • Granulated sugar
  • Brown sugar
  • Ground cinnamon
  • Unsalted butter
  • Pazazz apples
  • Cream cheese
  • Milk
  • Vanilla extract
  • Powdered sugar

About the ingredients

You can use canned crescent roll dough or sheets of canned crescent dough.

The sheets of dough are a little easier to work with because you don't need to press the crescent roll perforations together.

The butter and cream cheese should be softened before making the recipe.

Step by step instructions

1. Roll out the crescent roll dough.

Place a large piece of parchment paper on a flat work surface. Place both sheets of crescent roll dough slightly overlapping on the parchment paper and press the seams together.

2. Top with the cinnamon sugar butter.

In a medium bowl, combine softened butter with granulated sugar, brown sugar and ground cinnamon. Spread this mixture evenly across the crescent roll dough.

3. Add the apples.

Core and thinly slice one Pazazz apple, then place the slices on top of the cinnamon sugar butter.

4. Roll it up.

Roll the crescent roll dough up tightly over the apples and the cinnamon sugar butter into a tube.

5. Slice it in half.

Use a sharp knife to slice lengthwise through the middle of the cinnamon roll tube.

6. Separate the two halves.

Separate the two halves and make sure the cinnamon butter and apples are facing up.

7. Braid them together.

Carefully braid the two halves together, lifting one half over the other until it becomes one long braid.

8. Form the wreath.

Bring the two ends of the braid together to form the wreath. Transfer it to a baking sheet and bake at 350°F for 20-25 minutes, or until golden brown.
